## Platform

## Stack

Single static `index.html` with Three.js loaded from a CDN (ES module import map). No build step. Fonts and brand SVGs ship beside the page in `fonts/` and `assets/`.

## Users

The project is a concept redesign pitched to PitchPoint Pty Ltd (contact: Candice Cooke). The site it imagines speaks to PitchPoint's own clients: executives and business development leads in property development, planning and advisory, energy, infrastructure and technical industries. They struggle to get the attention of the right decision-makers, and they rely on referrals they don't trust for long-term growth.

## Product Purpose

PitchPoint does contract business development and sales administration. It runs outreach and corporate access campaigns (hosted lunches and dinners), one-to-one appointment-setting campaigns, and satellite representation from South Australia for Asia Pacific entry. It also produces positioning material (capability statements, pitch decks, tender material, corporate documentation) and offers PR, marketing and communications. The site succeeds when a prospective client understands that PitchPoint puts them in the room with the right people, then calls or emails.

## Positioning

PitchPoint curates and runs private business development events and campaigns end to end: coordination, guest outreach and management. It is not lead-gen software and it is not a generalist agency. It is a contract BD team that takes a client from scattered contacts to a seat at the table, with the collateral to back them.

## Product Principles

1. Get them in the room. Every chapter moves the visitor closer to the right decision-maker.
2. Show the work, not adjectives. Use real engagements and real deliverables.
3. One point, sharply made. Restraint and focus are the brand.
4. Make the human contact obvious. The phone and email are the call to action.
