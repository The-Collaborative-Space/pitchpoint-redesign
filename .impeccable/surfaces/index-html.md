## Direction contract

THESIS: "The point." One gold point moves through a navy field of contacts and reorganises it into a room, a document and a project. The site refuses the Webflow default of a hero, accordions and a slider.
OWN-WORLD: Deep navy #13151d ground, warm smoke #f1ece7 type and dots, muted gold #a4946f for the point, the line and actions. Condensed bold uppercase display (Antonio standing in for Bison), DM Sans body, a six-spoke asterisk as divider and marker. Hairline-gold controls, square corners.
STORY: You're scattered and relying on referrals → PitchPoint curates the room (events, outreach) → arms you (positioning collateral) → amplifies you (PR) → proof in real projects → call Candice.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed WebGL field. Wordmark top-left with a Connect link top-right. A 4-line H1 at bottom-left, capped at 6rem by the display ceiling (the real line is too long for 3 lines at that cap), the real line "We're really good at contract business development and sales administration". Focus areas plus a gold "Connect" button and a "See how" link. The gold point glows right of centre and follows the cursor softly.
FORM: Assigned, pinned by the user (brief-pinned: brand world plus "the point" 3D scroll story); first on the list. No seed: the world is pinned by brand commitment and the concept was chosen by the user.
SIGNATURE: Scroll-scrubbed particle morph field→chaos→table→deck→pipeline→broadcast→asterisk, with a camera choreographed per chapter.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
